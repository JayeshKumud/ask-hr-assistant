NexaCore Europe Visa and Mobility Policy

Fictional RAG test document. Not legal, HR, tax, or immigration advice.

Purpose

This fictional policy provides a general framework for employee immigration and mobility within Europe. Each destination country has its own work-permit and residence rules. This is a RAG test document and not immigration or legal advice.

EU Blue Card

NexaCore may consider the EU Blue Card for eligible highly qualified employees where the destination country participates and the employee satisfies applicable professional, employment, salary, travel-document, insurance, and national requirements.

Country-Specific Routes

Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Italy, and other participating EU Member States may have national highly skilled-worker routes in addition to or instead of the EU Blue Card. Denmark and Ireland require separate national routes.

Eligibility

Global Mobility reviews destination country, role, qualifications, employment relationship, compensation, employee status, immigration history, and national requirements.

Process

Select destination; HR confirms company capability; Global Mobility identifies the route; compensation and role eligibility are checked; documents are collected; application is submitted; visa or residence formalities are completed; and right-to-work/local registration checks are performed.

Compensation

Salary must satisfy the destination country's immigration threshold where one exists and fit NexaCore's local salary framework. Compensation depends on role, level, skills, experience, location, market data, and legal requirements.

Relocation Support

Approved mobility packages may include eligible travel, temporary accommodation, shipment support, immigration assistance, and tax support. The exact package is determined before relocation.

Cross-Border Work

Permission to work in one European country does not automatically create a right to work in another. Employees must contact Global Mobility before changing work country.

Business Travel

Business travel is different from employment authorisation. Short business entry does not necessarily authorise local employment.

Dependants

Dependant immigration arrangements are assessed under destination-country rules. Company support depends on the approved mobility package.

Change of Country

Employees must not move their employment or permanent work location from one European country to another without prior approval.

Compliance

Employees must provide accurate documentation and report relevant immigration changes.
