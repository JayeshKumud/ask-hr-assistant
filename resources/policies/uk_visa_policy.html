NexaCore United Kingdom Visa and Employment Policy

Fictional RAG test document. Not legal, HR, tax, or immigration advice.

Purpose

This fictional policy describes NexaCore's UK immigration-support process. It is for RAG testing and is not legal or immigration advice.

Skilled Worker

NexaCore may sponsor eligible roles under the UK Skilled Worker route where the company is authorised to sponsor and the role and employee satisfy applicable requirements.

Eligibility

HR and Global Mobility review occupation, salary, qualifications, English-language requirement where applicable, sponsorship history, right-to-work status, and other relevant conditions.

Process

Business submits sponsorship request; HR confirms the role is suitable; compensation is checked; Global Mobility and immigration counsel prepare the case; sponsorship documentation is issued; employee submits the visa application; and right-to-work checks are completed.

Compensation

Sponsored employees must receive at least applicable legal salary requirements and the company's approved salary for the role. Compensation considers level, skills, experience, location, market data, internal equity, and immigration requirements.

Costs

The company may cover approved employer-sponsored immigration counsel and sponsorship costs according to the relevant package. Dependant applications and optional services may require separate approval.

Leave

Employees taking annual, sick, maternity, paternity, parental, or other approved leave remain subject to employment and immigration rules. HR should be contacted before extended unpaid absence.

Dependants

Dependant immigration support is assessed separately under the rules applicable at the time.

Change of Employer, Role or Location

An existing visa does not automatically permit work for a new employer, job, or location. HR and immigration counsel must assess changes before they occur.

Right to Work

NexaCore completes required right-to-work checks. Employees must maintain valid work authorisation.

Remote Work

International remote work requires prior HR and Global Mobility review because it can create immigration, tax, payroll, employment, and social-security obligations.
