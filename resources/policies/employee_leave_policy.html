Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 working days per leave year plus applicable public holidays. Part-time entitlement is prorated. Planned leave should normally be requested at least 10 calendar days in advance.

Personal Leave

Employees may take up to 5 paid personal days per calendar year for personal appointments, urgent family matters, relocation activities, or other short-term personal needs.

Sick Leave

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible. NexaCore provides up to 10 working days of company sick pay at 100% base salary per rolling 12-month period. Statutory or contractual arrangements apply thereafter.

Medical and Family Leave

Reasonable paid time may be available for essential medical or dental appointments where local law or company arrangements permit. Employees may request paid or unpaid dependant-care leave subject to applicable rules.

Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 paid working days may be granted following the death of an immediate family member.

Maternity Leave

Eligible employees may take maternity leave under applicable law. NexaCore provides 16 weeks at 100% base salary as a fictional company-enhanced benefit, followed by statutory or contractual maternity pay where applicable.

Paternity and Partner Leave

An eligible parent or partner may take up to 4 weeks. The first 2 weeks are paid at 100% base salary; the remaining 2 weeks follow statutory or contractual arrangements.

Adoption and Shared Parental Leave

A primary adopter may receive leave equivalent to the applicable maternity or primary-adopter entitlement. Shared parental leave is available where provided by local law.

Unpaid and Special Leave

Employees may request unpaid parental or other approved unpaid leave. No base salary is paid during approved unpaid leave. Neonatal-care and parental-bereavement leave are administered where applicable under local law.

Salary During Leave

Approved annual and company-paid personal leave are paid at 100% normal base salary. Company sick pay is paid at 100% base salary for the company-paid allowance. Maternity and paternity pay follow the company-enhanced terms above.

Return to Work

HR and the manager should agree a return-to-work plan before extended leave ends.
